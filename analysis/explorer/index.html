What goes with the gaps

Corrected on 25 November 2026. The earlier version showed seven criteria picked after seeing the scores, ranked the causes without measuring them, mislabelled several run measures and left out that DeepSeek ran at a higher reasoning effort. The scores and gaps did not change.

DeepSeek leads by ; derived criteria carry of it, depending on how they are sorted

DeepSeek's mean score over both pipelines, minus the mean of Haiku and Luna, split by the kind of criterion it comes from. Derived criteria need a result the report must work out itself: an estimate or calculation for this case, a forecast, a verdict or a proof.

Two agents sorted all 139 criteria from the questions, criteria and weights, without scores, reports or model names, and agreed on every one. They are two instances of one model, and the sorting rule was written after the scores were known. Moving one or two criteria between the groups changes the derived share of the lead from (drawn above) to or . Why the models differ on these criteria was not tested.

The lead is not a comparison of models alone. DeepSeek ran at high reasoning effort (its API maps medium to high) and the other two at medium; its search had no tool cap and its researchers a larger output limit. It is best in of 12 question and pipeline cells, and below the mean of the other two on Q1 and Q2.
